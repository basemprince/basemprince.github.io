Thinking Out Loud

This page is a place for ideas that do not fit neatly into a portfolio: questions about consciousness, physics, human nature, society, and the odd edges where science, philosophy, and everyday experience overlap.

I want this section to be curious rather than dogmatic. Some notes may become polished essays over time; others may stay as sketches, questions, or attempts to clarify what I think.
